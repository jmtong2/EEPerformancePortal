Use the SUMMARY Campaign TEMPLATE.xlsx: sheet INFO (month and "as of" date), sheets Curing and Recovery (one row per telecollector), and optionally HOLIDAYS and LEADERS (TL / OM / GM). Older files like SUMMARY Campaign.xlsx and files exported from this portal also work.

Month, "As of" Date & Holidays

Targets (On Track Figures) = monthly figure ÷ business days in the month × business days elapsed up to the "as of" date. Business days = Monday–Friday minus the holidays below. Imports and daily entries move the "as of" date forward automatically.

KPI Rate Settings

Each metric is scored against its target (actual ÷ target, capped), then combined using the weights. Weights must add up to 100 for each bucket. While To Retain is blank, Provision is left out and the other weights are re-scaled.
